## When you need one

You do **not** need a commercial licence to make maps with MoboMap – for yourself, your company,
clients, teaching, publications or presentations – or to use or self-host it unchanged.

You **do** need one (or must comply with the AGPL) if you, for example:

- build MoboMap, or a modified version of it, into a closed-source product or internal tool you distribute,
- offer MoboMap or a modified version as a hosted service or SaaS without publishing your source code,
- white-label, rebrand or resell it, or bundle it with proprietary design systems, data or templates,
- need terms the AGPL does not offer (warranty, indemnity, support, custom licensing).

## What a commercial licence can include

- use without the AGPL source-code and network obligations,
- private changes and custom design systems kept confidential,
- support, custom features, styles, datasets or integrations by arrangement.
